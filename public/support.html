Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no login; results, streaks and progress stay on your device.

Can I stop the usage and crash data? Yes: Settings → Privacy, switch off Share usage data. The game keeps working the same. Details in the privacy policy.

Are there ads or purchases? No. The game is free and complete, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

How do I move my progress to a new phone? Progress is stored only on the device, so it cannot be transferred. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The daily reminder does not arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for Daily Math in the system settings and that the reminder is on in the app’s settings.

Reporting a problem

Send the email above with your device model, OS version and what you were doing. You can also use Settings → Support in the app, which opens an email with the details filled in.
